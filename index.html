Victoria Melnikova

Head of New Business at Evil Martians

X LinkedIn GitHub Email

Victoria Melnikova brings in new business at Evil Martians, the design and engineering firm behind Decagon, bolt.new, Tines and hundreds of early-stage startups. Since 2020 she has signed over 30 new clients totaling $2.6M in contracts.

Every year 40 early-stage startups bring her their go-to-market problems: positioning, developer experience, and finding the first paying customers.

In San Francisco, she hosts Dev Propulsion Labs, a podcast about the business of developer tools with 100K+ views and over 40 episodes with founders of Supabase, 37signals, WorkOS, and CodeRabbit. She's spoken at AI Engineer World's Fair in 2025 and 2026 and written for TechCrunch.

Victoria went to boarding school for girls in Russia, then studied Economics and Portuguese at Smith College. She's lived in England, Brazil, Portugal, and the US. She's a mom, a wife, a women-in-tech advocate, and a self-described Russian Ipanema girl.

If you have an opportunity to discuss, book a 15-minute call.
